A residence permit is what lets you stay in China beyond a short visit, and for an importer it is the document that keeps you able to travel, sign and follow up on shipments. The steps below are the order you perform them. No figure in this guide is verified, so treat every timing question as something to confirm with the authority handling your case before you rely on it.

## Before you start

Confirm which permit category fits your situation. The category is tied to why you are in China, and an importer's application is usually built on a work or business basis rather than a family one. Ask the authority that will receive your application which category applies to you before you collect documents.
Check that your passport has room and that your current entry status allows you to apply from inside China. If it does not, you may need to leave and re-enter on the correct visa first. This is the step people skip, and it is the one that sends them back to the start.

## Assemble the documents

Build the file in the order the application form lists it. Expect the form itself, your passport, photographs to the stated specification, and the supporting document that matches your category. For an importer that supporting document usually comes from your employer or your own registered company, and it has to be current.
Keep originals and copies separate and labelled. Offices commonly ask for one and then the other, and a labelled folder saves a return trip. Do not laminate anything and do not sign forms until you are in front of the officer if the form says so.

## Lodge the application

Go to the entry and exit administration office that covers where you live, not the one nearest your supplier or your hotel. Bring the whole file. You will be photographed and fingerprinted at the counter if that has not already been done for this application.
Keep the receipt you are given. It is your proof that you applied and it is what you show if anyone asks about your status while the application is being processed.

## While it is being processed

Do not let your current permission lapse while you wait. If your existing stay permission is close to running out, ask at the counter what your position is before it does, and follow whatever instruction you are given.
Avoid international travel during processing unless the office has told you it is fine. Leaving can invalidate an application made inside China.

## Collect the permit and keep it valid

Collect the permit in person when notified and check every field on it before you leave the counter: name spelling, passport number, category and validity dates. A wrong character in your name causes problems at the bank and at the border.
Note when the permit expires and start the renewal process before that date, not after. Renewal is a fresh application, so the document list applies again. Keep a copy of the permit and of your registration of residence where you can find them.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-09; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
