## Separate the event from your relocation

The CIIE is an exhibition. Your relocation runs on permits, housing and registration, and the exhibition does not change those requirements.
Decide first whether you are attending as a visitor, taking part as an exhibitor, or simply arriving in the same period. Each path has a different set of forms and a different office behind it.

## Where the real numbers come from

Ask the organiser or your host organisation for the current schedule and the current fee schedule in writing. Ask which platform handles registration and which office handles your visa or residence paperwork.
Keep the replies. When a figure appears in a chat message or a forwarded post, check it against the official channel before you pay or book anything.

## Build in slack around the event

Exhibition periods concentrate demand for hotels, flights and appointments. Book refundable where you can, and keep your permit and housing tasks on their own timeline rather than tying them to the event.
If a step depends on a document you do not yet hold, do that step after the document arrives, not before.
