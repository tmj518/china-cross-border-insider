Setting up Alipay or WeChat Pay as a foreigner is straightforward, but the details matter. This guide walks you through the actual steps that work, from downloading the app to making your first payment. You'll avoid common pitfalls like failed verification or card rejection.

## Before You Start

You need a passport that is valid for the duration of your stay, a foreign mobile number that can receive SMS, and a Visa or Mastercard issued outside China. Some users report that Amex works, but it is less reliable.
Both apps are available in English, but the interface may default to Chinese. You can switch the language in the settings after installation.

## Install and Register

Download Alipay or WeChat from your app store. For WeChat, you must also register a WeChat account first, which requires a phone number and a verification code.
For Alipay, you can register directly with your foreign phone number. The app will ask for your name and passport details during setup.

## Verify Your Identity

Both apps require identity verification to use payment features. You will need to photograph your passport and, in some cases, take a selfie for facial recognition.
Verification can take a few minutes or up to a few days. If it fails, check that your passport photo is clear and that your name matches exactly what is on the card you will link.

## Link Your Foreign Card

Go to the payment settings and choose 'Add Card' or 'Link a Card'. Enter your card number, expiry, and CVV. The app may send a small temporary charge to verify the card, which is refunded.
Some cards are declined because the issuing bank blocks international transactions. Contact your bank before you travel to enable overseas use.

## Make Your First Payment

At a merchant, scan the QR code or show your payment code. For amounts above a certain threshold, you may be asked to enter your payment PIN or verify with your fingerprint.
If a transaction fails, check that your card has sufficient funds and that the merchant accepts foreign cards. Some small vendors only accept local payment methods.
