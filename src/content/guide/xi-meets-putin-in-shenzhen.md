A meeting between Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen is a news event, not a visa category. It can still affect your relocation through temporary security zones, traffic controls and office closures. This procedure tells you what to check and do, in order, without quoting any figure that could not be verified.

## What this event is and is not

A summit meeting is a diplomatic event. It does not create a new permit, change your eligibility, or replace any application you already have in progress.
Do not treat announcements about the meeting as instructions for your own paperwork. Your application is governed by the rules of the office handling it, not by the summit.
Because no figure in this topic could be verified against a source, this guide gives you no dates, counts or amounts. Verify every timing detail with the office that issues your document.

## Check whether your appointments or routes are affected

High-profile visits can bring temporary road closures, security perimeters around venues and hotels, and short-notice changes to public transport.
Offices near event areas may close or shorten hours. Check the official channel of the specific office you are dealing with, not general news.
If you have an appointment, confirm it directly with the office before you travel. Keep the confirmation with you.

## Protect your application timeline

Assume processing can slow around the event and plan to submit before any deadline rather than on it.
Keep copies of every receipt, appointment slip and submission confirmation. If an office closes, a dated record helps you explain a missed step.
Do not let a permit expire while you wait for news about the summit. Renew or extend before the permit expires, using the office's own published rules.

## Keep your documents and contact details current

Make sure your address registration and phone number are up to date with the office that holds your file, so notices reach you.
Carry your passport and residence documents when moving around event areas, since checks can increase.
Save the official contact channel for your issuing office and use it for any question about your case.

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-19; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
