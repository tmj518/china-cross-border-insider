A residence permit is what lets you stay in China beyond the entry stamped in your passport. The order of steps matters more than the paperwork itself, because one wrong move can mean leaving the country to start again. This is the procedure only; confirm current requirements with your local exit-entry office or your employer's immigration handler before you act.

## Before you apply

Your permit is tied to the purpose you entered for, so the first task is confirming which category applies to you and that your supporting documents match it. Your employer, school or host usually holds the paperwork that proves the purpose, and you should have it in hand before you queue anywhere.
Check that your passport has room and that your name, date of birth and passport number are identical across every document. Mismatches between a passport and a supporting letter are a common reason an application is refused at the counter.

## The order of steps

Most applicants first register their address with the local public security station, then attend the exit-entry administration for the permit itself. Some cities require a medical check before the permit application, and some require it only for certain categories. Ask your local office which applies to you rather than assuming.
Do not let your entry visa or current permit lapse while you are assembling documents. If it is close to expiring, deal with the extension or conversion first, because an overstay is handled separately and can affect the permit decision.

## At the counter

Bring originals and copies of everything, your passport, and the photographs the office specifies. Expect to give fingerprints and to be photographed on site. Keep every receipt you are handed: the receipt is your proof of application while the permit is being processed, and you may need it to travel or to show an employer.
If an officer asks for a document you do not have, ask for the requirement in writing or note the officer's name and the window. Returning with the right paper is faster than arguing.

## After you collect it

Check the permit before you leave the building: the category, the dates and your passport details. Report any error at the counter immediately, because corrections after you leave are slower.
Note the expiry date and set your own reminder well before it. Renewal is a fresh application, not an automatic extension, and the same document trail applies.
