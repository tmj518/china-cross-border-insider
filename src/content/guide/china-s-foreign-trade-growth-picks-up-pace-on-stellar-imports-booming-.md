You are moving to China because of trade work, and the news about import growth and AI demand tells you the sector is busy, not what your own paperwork will require. Nothing in this guide is legal, tax or immigration advice, and no figure here has been checked against a source. Treat every step as something to confirm with the office or employer handling your case before you act on it.

## What the trade headlines do and do not tell you

Reports that trade growth has picked up, driven by imports and demand tied to AI, describe the country's aggregate numbers. They do not describe your employer, your city, your role or your permit category.
Do not use those reports to plan a budget, a start date or a housing decision. Use them only as background for conversations with the people who will actually file your papers.

## Fix your purpose and your sponsor first

Before you book anything, write down in plain terms what you will be doing in China and which entity in China is responsible for you. The permit route follows from that answer, not the other way round.
Ask the sponsor to name the person who will handle your application and to confirm in writing which documents they will prepare and which ones you must supply.

## Gather documents before you commit to travel

Document legalisation, translation and authentication take time you cannot compress by paying more, so start them while you are still abroad. Keep scanned copies of everything you send, and keep the originals with you rather than in checked luggage.
If any certificate has an expiry printed on it, check it against your intended arrival and renew it before you travel rather than after.

## On arrival, register and keep records

Complete the local registration steps your sponsor or landlord tells you about within the window they give you, and keep the receipts. Keep your passport, permit and registration papers together and carry copies when you travel inside the country.
Note the expiry date on every document you receive and put a reminder in your calendar well before it, because renewals start from the document, not from your plans.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-12; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
