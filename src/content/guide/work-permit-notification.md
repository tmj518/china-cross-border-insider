The work permit notification is a separate document you must obtain before you can apply for the Z visa. It is issued by the science and technology authority, not by the visa office. This guide explains the procedure for one application.

## Understand the Two Documents

The work permit notification and the Z visa are two different documents held by two different authorities. The notification is issued by the science and technology authority, while the visa is issued by the Chinese embassy or consulate.
You need the notification first. Without it, you cannot apply for the Z visa.

## Know the Permit Classification

The work permit notification is classified into categories. The classification determines what your employer must prove to obtain it.
Your employer will need to provide different supporting documents depending on the classification. Confirm with your employer which classification applies to your role.

## Employer Applies for the Notification

Your employer initiates the application with the local science and technology authority. You do not apply for this document yourself.
The employer submits the required proof based on the classification. This may include your qualifications, work experience, and other documents.

## Receive and Use the Notification

Once approved, the employer receives the work permit notification and gives it to you.
You use this notification to apply for the Z visa at the Chinese embassy or consulate in your home country. The visa office will not issue the visa without it.

## After Arrival: Convert to Residence Permit

After you arrive in China with the Z visa, the work permit notification becomes the basis for a residence permit.
You must apply for the residence permit with the local public security bureau within the time allowed after arrival. The residence permit allows you to stay and work legally.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-29; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
