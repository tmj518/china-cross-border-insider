This guide walks you through the China work permit application process as a foreign national relocating for employment. It covers the sequence from your employer's initial filings to your physical permit card, without quoting any fees or processing times. Each step is an action you or your employer takes in order.

## Before You Start

Your employer must be authorized to hire foreign staff and will act as your sponsor throughout the process. You will need a valid passport, a clean criminal record from your home country, and a medical check that meets Chinese requirements.
Confirm your job category (A, B, or C) with your employer, as this determines the documents and approval path. Your employer will typically handle the online applications, but you must provide accurate personal information and signed documents.

## The Application Sequence

The process has two main phases: first, your employer applies for a Work Permit Notification (foreigner's work permit notice) from the local government, and second, you use that notice to apply for a Z visa at a Chinese embassy or consulate abroad.
After you enter China with the Z visa, you must convert it to a residence permit and receive the physical work permit card. Each phase requires specific documents and approvals, and you cannot skip ahead.

## After Arrival in China

Once you arrive, you have a limited window to complete the post-arrival procedures. Your employer will help you apply for the work permit card and the residence permit, which are separate but linked.
You must also register your address with the local police within the required period after moving in. Keep all your documents together, as you will need them for multiple steps.
