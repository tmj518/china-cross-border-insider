This guide walks you through the application process for a China permanent residence permit. It is not legal or immigration advice, and requirements can change. Follow the steps in order to prepare and submit your application.

## Before You Start

Confirm you meet the basic eligibility categories, such as investment, employment, family reunion, or special contribution. Each category has its own documentary requirements.
Gather your original documents and prepare photocopies. You will need a valid passport, visa or residence permit, and supporting evidence for your category.
Check with the local Exit and Entry Administration Department for any category-specific forms or additional requirements.

## Prepare Your Application File

Complete the official application form for permanent residence. Obtain it from the local public security bureau or its website.
Compile all required supporting documents, including proof of your status (employment, investment, family relationship, or contribution).
Translate any non-Chinese documents into Chinese by a certified translation agency, and notarize them if required.
Prepare recent passport-sized photos that meet the specified standards.

## Submit and Track

Submit your application in person at the local Exit and Entry Administration Department. Some categories may require an interview.
Pay the application fee when you submit. The fee is non-refundable even if the application is rejected.
You will receive a receipt with a tracking number. Use it to check the status online or by phone.
If the authorities request additional documents, provide them within the given deadline.

## After Approval

When approved, you will be notified to collect your permanent residence permit. Bring your passport and the receipt.
Check the permit details for accuracy before leaving the office.
Renew or update the permit as required by law, and carry it with your passport when traveling.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-09; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
