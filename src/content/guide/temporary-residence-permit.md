A temporary residence permit is what you register for after you arrive, and the order of steps matters more than speed. This guide lists the procedure only. It contains no figures because none could be verified, and it is not legal, tax or immigration advice.

## Before you start

Check what your entry visa or permit already allows and what your local public security bureau office expects from someone in your situation. Requirements differ by city and by the reason you are in China.
Gather your passport, your entry documents and the address details of where you are staying. Your landlord or host may need to provide a registration form for the address.
Ask your employer, school or host whether they handle part of the application for you. Many do, and going alone when they expected to file can slow things down.

## At the office

Go to the public security bureau office that covers your address, not the one nearest your workplace. Take the originals of everything you gathered, plus copies if the office asks for them.
Submit the application form and your documents at the counter. Keep the receipt they give you; you will need it to collect the permit or to show your status if asked before the permit is issued.
If the officer asks for something you do not have, ask them to write down exactly what is missing. Returning with the right document is faster than guessing.

## After you submit

Your passport may be held while the application is processed. Ask when you can collect it and whether you can travel in the meantime; plan around the answer.
Collect the permit in person when the office tells you it is ready, and check the details on it before you leave the counter. Report any error immediately rather than later.
Note the expiry date and set your own reminder before it, because renewal is a new application and not an automatic extension.

## If your situation changes

If you move address, change employer or change school, ask the office whether your permit needs to be updated. Do this before the change takes effect where possible.
If you plan to leave China and return, check that your permit and visa still allow re-entry before you book travel.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-09; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
