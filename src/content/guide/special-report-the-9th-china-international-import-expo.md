The China International Import Expo (CIIE) is held annually in Shanghai, and as a new resident you can attend as a trade visitor. This guide walks you through registration, travel, and entry so you can plan your visit without surprises.

## Before You Register

Check your residence permit and passport validity. You will need both documents to complete the visitor registration and to enter the exhibition hall.
Decide whether you are attending as a professional buyer or as a general visitor. The registration process and access differ, and your choice affects which halls you can enter.

## Registration Process

Visit the official CIIE website and create an account using your passport details. The registration system opens several months before the event and closes before the expo begins.
Upload a recent passport-style photo and a copy of your residence permit. The system will ask you to select your visit dates and the type of badge you need.
Wait for the approval email. Once approved, you will receive a confirmation with a QR code. Print the QR code or save it on your phone—you will need it to collect your physical badge on site.

## Travel and Accommodation

Book your hotel in Shanghai well in advance, as prices rise sharply during the expo period. Choose a hotel near a metro line that connects to the National Exhibition and Convention Center.
Plan to arrive in Shanghai at least one day before your first scheduled visit. This gives you time to collect your badge and familiarize yourself with the venue layout.

## On the Day of Your Visit

Bring your passport, residence permit, and the QR code to the badge collection counters at the venue. The counters are located outside the main entrance and are clearly marked.
Expect security checks similar to airport procedures. Do not bring large bags or prohibited items; use the luggage storage services if needed.
Inside the venue, follow the crowd flow and use the official app to locate exhibitors. The expo is vast, so prioritize the halls that match your interests.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-11; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
