A tax refund in China is a procedure with a fixed order, and most of the cost of getting it wrong is time. This guide walks through the steps in the order you perform them. It is not legal, tax or immigration advice, and no figure in this topic could be verified against a source, so treat every amount and deadline as something you confirm yourself.

## Before you start: what the refund actually is

A refund is money withheld from you that turns out not to be owed, returned through a claim. It is not a benefit you apply for and it is not automatic. You claim it, and the claim is assessed.
Because the fact sheet for this topic is empty, this guide deliberately contains no amounts, no deadlines and no thresholds. Where a step would normally carry a number, you will be told what to confirm and with whom, not what the number is.

## Gather your documents before you file anything

The claim is assessed on documents, so the documents come first. Assemble your identity document, your permit or visa showing your status, your employment or income records, and the withholding records your employer or payer issued to you.
Keep copies of everything you submit. If the claim is queried, you will be asked to show the same documents again, and having them ready is the difference between a short exchange and a long one.

## File through the channel that matches your situation

There is more than one way to file, and which one applies to you depends on your status and on how the income was paid. Confirm the correct channel with the tax authority or with the person who handles your payroll before you submit, rather than after.
Submit once, through one channel. Filing in two places at the same time creates a reconciliation problem that takes longer to fix than the original claim.

## Respond to queries and keep the record

After you file, the claim may be assessed without further contact or it may be queried. Answer queries with the documents you already gathered, and answer them in the channel the query came through.
Keep the filed claim, the query and your response together in one place. When you leave or change employers, that record is what you hand over or refer back to.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-02; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
