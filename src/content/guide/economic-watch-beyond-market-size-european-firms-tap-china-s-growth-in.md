This procedure covers the actions you take to apply for a China work permit as a relocating professional. It contains no figures because no figure in this topic could be verified against a source. Treat it as a checklist of actions, not as legal, tax or immigration advice.

## What this procedure covers

This is the sequence of actions for one work permit application. It does not cover dependant visas, permanent residence or tax registration.
Because no figure in this topic could be verified, every step below describes timing and quantities in words rather than numbers. Confirm each item with your employer and the local exit-entry administration before you act.

## Before you start

Confirm with your employer which office will handle the application and who submits it. In many cases the employer files on your behalf, so your job is to supply documents and attend appointments.
Check that your passport has blank pages and remains valid for the period your employer requires. Replace it first if it does not, because a new passport means restarting document collection.

## Documents you will be asked for

Expect to provide a passport-style photograph, your passport, your highest degree certificate and a criminal record certificate from each country where you lived for a substantial period. Some documents need authentication or legalisation before submission.
Non-English documents usually need a translation. Ask the receiving office which translator or notary they accept before you pay for one.

## After the permit is issued

The work permit is not the end of the process. You then apply for a residence permit at the exit-entry administration, and you register with the local police station after you move into your address.
Keep every receipt and approval notice. You will be asked for them again at renewal, and at the residence permit stage.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-09; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
