You are hiring or relocating staff into China and need to arrange health cover. This procedure walks through what to check and in what order, using only what can be confirmed with the insurer and the local authorities. It is not legal, tax or immigration advice, and no figure in this topic could be verified against a source, so none is given here.

## What to settle before you contact any insurer

Decide who the cover is for and where they will be based. Staff on local Chinese contracts, staff seconded from abroad and dependants are usually treated differently, so list each person and their situation before you shop.
Confirm what the employment contract and any collective agreement already promise. If cover is promised, the promise sets your minimum; if it is silent, you are choosing a benefit, not filling a gap.
Check whether your staff will be enrolled in China's public medical insurance scheme through the local social insurance registration. Public enrolment and private cover are separate questions, and the answer changes what private cover needs to do.

## How to shortlist providers

Ask providers whether they cover staff based in China directly or only through an international plan with a China network. Get the answer in writing, naming the hospitals and clinics in the cities where your staff actually live.
Ask how claims are paid: direct billing at the hospital, reimbursement to the employee, or both. Ask which hospitals in your staff's cities accept direct billing, because a plan that only reimburses leaves the employee to pay first.
Ask how the provider handles pre-existing conditions, maternity, dental, vision and evacuation. Do not accept a verbal answer; ask for the policy wording and read the exclusions yourself.

## What to verify before you sign

Confirm the provider is licensed to sell the product in the place where the policy is issued, and ask who the regulated entity is. If the policy is issued outside China, ask how that affects an employee who needs care inside China.
Check the renewal terms and what happens if an employee leaves, transfers city or adds a dependant mid-term. Ask whether premiums are reviewed at renewal and on what basis.
Ask for the claims process in plain steps: who the employee calls, what they submit, and how long reimbursement takes. Write it down and give it to staff before their start date.

## What to hand to each employee

Give each employee the policy summary, the card or app details, the list of direct-billing hospitals near them, and the claims contact. Do this before their first working day, not after.
Tell them what the plan does not cover and what they must pay themselves. Staff who discover an exclusion at the hospital counter are the ones who call you.
Keep a record of who is enrolled, when cover starts and when it ends, and review it whenever someone joins, leaves or moves city.
