This guide walks you through the procedure for applying under Council Regulation (EU) 2026/382. It covers what to prepare, where to file, and what to expect after submission. The steps are ordered as you would perform them.

## Before You Start

Check that you meet the eligibility conditions set out in the regulation. Gather the supporting documents listed in the official application form.
Make copies of all documents. You will need to submit originals or certified copies, depending on the authority's requirements.

## Filling the Application

Complete the official application form for the regulation. Fill every field accurately; incomplete forms are returned without review.
Attach the required evidence. If any document is in a language other than the official language of the country where you apply, include a certified translation.

## Submitting and Following Up

Submit the application to the designated authority in the country where you reside or intend to reside. Use the submission method specified in the instructions.
After submission, you will receive an acknowledgment. Keep it safe; you may need it for follow-up inquiries.
The authority may request additional information. Respond promptly to avoid delays. You will be notified of the decision in writing.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-10; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
