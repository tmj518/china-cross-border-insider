This guide walks you through the process of using China's visa-free transit policy. You must verify your own eligibility before booking, as rules change. Follow these steps in order to avoid problems at immigration.

## Confirm Your Eligibility

Check that your nationality is on the approved list for the visa-free transit program. The list is published by China's immigration authority and can change.
You must hold a valid passport with at least six months of remaining validity, and a confirmed onward ticket to a third country or region (not your country of origin).
Your itinerary must include entering China from one country and departing to another, with your entire stay within the allowed period.

## Plan Your Itinerary

Book flights that arrive at and depart from ports that participate in the visa-free transit program. Not all airports and seaports are included.
Ensure your onward ticket is to a destination outside mainland China, and that you have a visa or entry permit for that destination if required.
Keep your travel documents and tickets ready for inspection at immigration.

## At the Border

Go to the visa-free transit counter at immigration, not the regular foreigner lanes. Present your passport, onward ticket, and any required documents.
The officer will stamp your passport with a transit permit. Check the exit date on the stamp; you must leave before that date.
Do not overstay. Overstaying can result in fines and a ban from entering China.

## During Your Stay

You are allowed to stay only in the area specified by the transit policy, which is usually the administrative region where you entered. Check the boundaries.
You cannot work or study during this transit stay. It is for tourism or short visits only.
Keep your passport with you at all times, as police may ask to see it.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-09; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
