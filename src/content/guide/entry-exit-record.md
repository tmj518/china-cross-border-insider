The entry and exit record is the document that shows the dates you entered and left China. Banks, schools and residence-permit desks ask for it when they want a stay history. This guide covers how to request it and what to expect from the document itself.

## What the record is

The record is issued by the exit-entry authority. It lists the dates you entered and left the country, drawn from the authority's own records.
It states facts only. It does not state the purpose of your travel, so do not expect it to explain why you were in China or what you were doing there.
Because it comes from the authority rather than from you, it carries more weight than a personal list of dates or a stack of stamps.

## Who asks for it

A bank may ask for it when opening an account or reviewing your history. A school may ask for it when enrolling you or a family member. A residence-permit desk may ask for it when it wants a stay history before processing your application.
Ask the office requesting it what period they need covered, so you request the right record the first time.

## How to request it

You can request the record in person at the exit-entry authority or through the official platform. Either way, the applicant proves identity, so bring the identification the office asks for and be ready to show it.
If you are requesting on someone else's behalf, check with the office first what they require, since the applicant is the one who proves identity.
Keep the record you receive with your other immigration paperwork. You may be asked for it again.
