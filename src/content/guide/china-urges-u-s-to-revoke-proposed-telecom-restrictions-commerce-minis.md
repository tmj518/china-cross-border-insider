China's commerce ministry has urged the United States to revoke proposed telecom restrictions. If you are relocating, the practical question is whether those proposals touch your phone service, your devices or your work. This guide gives you the order to check things in. It is not legal, tax or immigration advice.

## What the dispute is about

The commerce ministry statement is a government response to proposed U.S. restrictions on telecom. It is a diplomatic position, not a rule you can act on yet.
Proposals are not the same as rules in force. Until a measure is final and published, you cannot know its scope, its start date or who it covers. Treat any figure you see quoted about this dispute as unverified.

## Check your own exposure first

Before you change plans, list what you actually rely on: the phone number you use for banking and verification codes, the carrier behind it, and any work accounts tied to it.
Then ask your carrier and your employer's IT contact whether the proposal, if finalized, would affect your line or your devices. Ask them to put the answer in writing.

## Do not act on headlines

Do not cancel a contract, port a number or buy replacement hardware because of a proposed measure. A proposal can be withdrawn, narrowed or delayed, and reversing a cancellation usually costs you.
Do not sign a new long-term service contract on the assumption that the proposal will or will not happen. Keep your commitments short until the position is final.

## Keep your records and a fallback

Save the commerce ministry statement and any carrier or employer replies you receive, with the date you received them. If a rule is later finalized, these records show what you knew and when.
Keep a second way to receive verification codes and work messages that does not depend on a single carrier. Test it before you need it.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-26; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
