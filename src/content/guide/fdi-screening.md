Foreign investment screening in China is handled through the foreign investment information reporting system and, for certain sectors, through sector-specific review. The exact thresholds, timelines and fees are not published in a single verified source, so this guide gives you the order of actions only. Treat every figure you encounter elsewhere as something to confirm with the authority handling your file.

## What screening covers

Screening applies to foreign investment in sectors that are restricted or require special management, and to transactions that may affect national security. The scope is defined by the negative list and by sector rules, not by the size of your investment alone.
Because the fact sheet for this topic is empty, this guide does not state any threshold, fee, processing period or penalty. Confirm those with the commerce authority or the market regulation bureau where your entity will be registered.

## Before you file

Identify the sector of your proposed business and check it against the current negative list. If your sector is on the list, screening is part of your path; if it is not, you still file information reports but the review is lighter.
Assemble your corporate documents, the identity documents of the ultimate beneficial owner, and a description of the business you intend to conduct. Have translations ready in the form the receiving office requires.

## Filing and after

File through the foreign investment information reporting system or through the window the local commerce authority directs you to. Keep the receipt or acknowledgement; you will need it when you register the entity and when you open bank accounts.
If the authority asks for supplementary materials, respond before any deadline they set in writing. Do not assume silence means approval; confirm the status through the channel they gave you.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-17; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
