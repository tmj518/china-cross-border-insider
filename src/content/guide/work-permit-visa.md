This guide walks you through the process of getting a work permit visa in China, from securing a job offer to receiving your residence permit. It is based on general procedures and not legal advice.

## Before You Start

You must have a job offer from a Chinese employer who is willing to sponsor your work permit. Your employer will initiate most of the application process.
Ensure your passport is valid for the entire duration of your intended stay, as the permit will be tied to your passport's validity.

## Employer Applies for Work Permit

Your employer applies for a Work Permit Notification Letter from the local government authorities. This letter is required before you can apply for a visa.
You will need to provide your employer with your passport copy, CV, and any relevant qualifications or certificates.

## Apply for the Visa

Once you receive the Work Permit Notification Letter, you apply for a Z visa (work visa) at the Chinese embassy or consulate in your home country or current residence.
Submit your passport, the notification letter, a completed visa application form, and a recent photo. The visa allows you to enter China for work purposes.

## After Arrival in China

Within the validity period of your Z visa, you must enter China and then complete the remaining steps to convert your visa to a residence permit.
Your employer will assist with the registration and medical check requirements.

## Finalize Your Work Permit and Residence Permit

Undergo a medical examination at a designated hospital in China, as required for work permit issuance.
Your employer submits the application for your Work Permit Card and Residence Permit to the local authorities. You will receive these documents, which allow you to work and stay legally.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-09; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
