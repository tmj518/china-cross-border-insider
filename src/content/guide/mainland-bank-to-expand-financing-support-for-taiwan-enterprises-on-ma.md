Mainland banks have signalled wider financing support for Taiwan-owned enterprises operating on the mainland. The application itself is a sequence of confirmations, and the order matters: each step tells you whether the next one is worth doing. No figure in this guide is verified, so treat every number a bank or intermediary quotes as something you check at the counter, not something you accept from a brochure.

## Before you start

Confirm your enterprise is registered on the mainland and that your registration documents are current. The bank will ask for the entity, not for you personally.
Ask the branch which products the support programme covers and which it does not. Support announced at the policy level does not automatically mean every loan type at every branch.
Do not pay any intermediary who offers to arrange approval. Apply at the branch yourself or through your own authorised representative.

## What the bank will ask for

Expect requests for business registration, proof of Taiwan ownership or investment, financial statements, and evidence of what the money is for. Ask for the full list in writing at the first visit so you gather it once.
Ask whether the branch treats your application under the Taiwan-enterprise programme or as an ordinary commercial application. The two can have different documentation and different review paths.

## After you submit

Get a written acknowledgement of what you handed in and the name of the person handling it. Keep asking for the status in writing rather than by phone.
If the bank asks for more documents, supply them before any deadline it sets in writing. If you cannot, tell the handler before the deadline passes rather than after.

## If you are refused or offered different terms

Ask for the reason in writing. A refusal under the programme and a refusal on ordinary commercial grounds are different answers and lead to different next steps.
Before signing anything, read the fee schedule and the repayment terms yourself. Nothing here is legal, tax or immigration advice, and no figure in this guide has been verified against a source.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-26; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
