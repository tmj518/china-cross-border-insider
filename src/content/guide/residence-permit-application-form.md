The residence permit application form is the core document for your legal stay in China. Filling it out accurately saves you time and prevents complications. This guide walks you through each part of the form in the order you will encounter it.

## Before You Start

Gather your passport, visa, and any supporting documents such as your employment contract or enrollment letter. Have your host organization's details ready, including their official name and address.
Check the latest version of the form at your local Exit-Entry Administration Bureau or online. Forms change, and using an outdated one can cause rejection.

## Filling Out Personal Information

Write your name exactly as it appears in your passport, including any middle names. Use the same spelling and order as the passport.
Provide your date of birth in the format requested, typically year-month-day. Double-check the numbers against your passport.
List your nationality and any previous nationalities if applicable. Be consistent with your visa application.

## Address and Contact Details

Enter your current residential address in China, including the building and room number. This must match the address you registered with the local police station.
Provide a working phone number and email. Use a number you check regularly, as authorities may contact you for clarifications.

## Purpose and Duration of Stay

Select the purpose that matches your visa type, such as work, study, or family reunion. Do not mix purposes.
State the intended duration of stay. This should align with your supporting documents, such as the contract or enrollment period.
Indicate whether you plan to extend the permit later. Be honest, as this affects the validity period granted.

## Review and Submission

Read every field again before signing. A single typo can lead to a rejection or a request for a new form.
Sign the form in the designated place, using the same signature as in your passport. Submit it along with all required attachments to the immigration office.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-31; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
