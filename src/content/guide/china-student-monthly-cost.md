# What a month in China costs a student

This page answers one question: **if you are an international student in China, what does a month cost, and where does each number come from.** It covers Beijing, Shanghai and Guangzhou, and it gives you three lines: transport, food, and housing.

**One of those three lines has no official number, and this page leaves it blank rather than filling it.** That is the point of the page, so it is the first thing to say.

## The honest summary

| Line | Beijing | Shanghai | Guangzhou | Sourced? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transport, per trip | 3 CNY up to 6 km | 3 CNY up to 6 km | 2 CNY up to 4 km | Yes — official fare tables |
| Food, per month | 846 CNY | 846 CNY | 846 CNY | Partly — national urban average, not city |
| Dormitory, per month | 1,200 CNY | see below | 417 CNY | Yes — three universities, not a market average |

The food figure is the same in all three columns because the statistics are national. Presenting three different food numbers would mean inventing a city split the source does not contain.

## 1. Transport: the one line you can actually calculate

Each city prices a metro journey by distance, and each publishes the bands. Beijing and Shanghai both start at 3 CNY for the first six kilometres.

Beijing's table, from the municipal government's own fare page:

| Distance | Fare |
|---|---|
| Up to 6 km | 3 CNY |
| 6 to 12 km | 4 CNY |
| 12 to 22 km | 5 CNY |
| 22 to 32 km | 6 CNY |
| Capital Airport Express | 25 CNY fixed |

Shanghai charges 3 CNY for the first six kilometres and then 1 CNY for every additional ten.

Guangzhou starts lower — 2 CNY for the first four kilometres — and caps the whole network at 20 CNY.

**Work it out for your own commute:** find the band that matches the distance between your stop and your campus, then multiply by the number of trips you make in a month. A student commuting 30 minutes each way, twice a day, is usually in the middle bands, not the first one.

## 2. Food: a national average, and what it does not tell you

The national statistics office publishes household consumption by category. The food, tobacco and alcohol line for 2025 is 8631 CNY per person for the year nationally, and 10155 CNY for urban residents.

Divided by twelve, the urban figure is **846 CNY per person per month**. That is the only food number on this page, and three things are wrong with using it as your budget:

- it is an **average**, so half of urban residents spend less
- it includes **tobacco and alcohol**, which many students do not buy
- it is **national urban**, not Beijing, Shanghai or Guangzhou, where food costs more

Use it as a floor for a student cooking at home, and expect your own number to be higher in all three cities.

## 3. Housing: dormitory fees, three universities, no market average

No official city-wide market rent is published for Beijing, Shanghai or Guangzhou. What is published is what a university charges for its own rooms, which is a different number and, for a student, the one that matters.

Three universities publish a fee a reader can check:

| University | City | Room | Published fee | Per month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tsinghua University | Beijing | Double room | 40 CNY per day | 1,200 CNY |
| Sun Yat-sen University | Guangzhou | 19 sq m per student | 5,000 CNY per academic year | 417 CNY |
| Zhengzhou University | Zhengzhou | Single room | 21,900 CNY per academic year | 1,825 CNY |

Three things to read carefully, because they are where these numbers lie:

- **The units are not the same.** Tsinghua prices by the day, the other two by the academic year. The monthly column is derived — 40 × 30, and the annual fees ÷ 12 — and the assumption is 30 days to a month with a room occupied all year.
- **Sun Yat-sen publishes floor area per student, not room type.** 19 square metres per student is shared; 38 square metres is 10,000 CNY a year. The page does not say "single" or "double", so neither does this one.
- **Zhengzhou is not one of the three cities on this page.** It is here because Shanghai's universities publish their accommodation fees as embedded PDFs or inside application portals that could not be read and quoted. A Zhengzhou figure with a checkable source is worth more than a Shanghai figure without one.

**No official city-wide market rent is published for these cities. These are university-published dormitory fees, which is what a student actually pays.**

If you plan to live outside the dormitory, ask the international student office which districts students actually rent in, then treat any figure you find as a starting point to verify rather than a cost.

## 4. When this page is wrong

- **You live outside the dormitory.** The housing line is blank, so your total is missing its largest component.
- **You are not in Beijing, Shanghai or Guangzhou.** The fare bands above are city-specific; other cities price differently.
- **The statistics are revised.** The food figure is an annual release, and later revisions change it.
- **You travel by bus, bike or car.** The transport line covers the metro only.
