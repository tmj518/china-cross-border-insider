The China-ASEAN comprehensive strategic partnership shapes trade, travel, and investment rules. This guide explains how it affects your relocation and what steps to take. It is not legal or immigration advice.

## Why the Partnership Matters for Relocation

The partnership simplifies some cross-border procedures for ASEAN nationals, but specific benefits depend on your home country and purpose of stay. Expect smoother customs clearance for personal goods and faster processing for certain business visas.
Check with the Chinese embassy in your home country for current bilateral agreements, as these change over time.

## Before You Depart

Gather your passport, proof of employment or enrollment, and any documents proving ASEAN residency. Verify that your visa category matches your intended activity—work, study, or family reunion.
If you plan to bring a vehicle or large equipment, review ASEAN-China trade facilitation rules for temporary import permits.

## Upon Arrival in China

Register your address with the local police station within the legally required period. Keep your passport and visa copies handy for registration.
For business registration, the partnership may reduce paperwork for ASEAN investors, but you still need a local sponsor or partner for most ventures.

## Staying Compliant

Renew your residence permit before it expires. The partnership does not override China's domestic immigration rules.
If you change employers or address, update your permit promptly to avoid fines.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-24; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
