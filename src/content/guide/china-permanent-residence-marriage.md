Marriage to a Chinese citizen is one of the categories for permanent residence in China. The application has specific conditions and is decided by the immigration authority, so meeting the conditions does not guarantee approval. The permit you receive is renewable, meaning your status is not permanent in the sense of never expiring.

## Understand the Conditions

The conditions for this category concern the duration of your marriage and the time you have spent living in China. You must meet both to be eligible to apply.
The immigration authority reviews each application individually. Meeting the conditions is necessary but not sufficient for approval.

## Prepare Your Documents

Gather evidence of your marriage, such as a marriage certificate, and proof of your residence in China, like residence permits and entry-exit stamps.
You will also need your passport, a valid visa or residence permit, and any documents the immigration authority requests. Make copies of everything.

## Submit Your Application

Apply at the local immigration authority office where you live. You may need to book an appointment in advance.
Submit the completed application form and all supporting documents. The authority will process your application and may ask for additional information.

## Receive and Renew Your Permit

If approved, you will receive a permanent residence permit. This permit has a validity period and must be renewed before it expires.
Renewal requires a new application. Keep track of the expiry date and start the renewal process in advance to avoid a gap in your status.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-29; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
