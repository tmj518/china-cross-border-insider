The Z visa is issued for employment in China. It is not the final stay document, because it is converted into a residence permit after arrival. The employer's work permit notification must be in place before the visa application is submitted.

## How the Z visa and residence permit fit together

A Z visa is issued for employment and supports entry for that job. After arrival, the employee applies to convert it into a residence permit.
The visa and the residence permit are two documents issued by two different authorities. The visa is issued by the relevant Chinese visa authority abroad; the residence permit is issued by the local exit-entry administration.

## Work permit notification comes first

The employer must provide the work permit notification before the employee applies for the Z visa. The notification comes before the visa application, not after.
Wait until the notification is available before arranging the rest of the visa submission.

## Medical examination

The medical examination is part of the Z visa application. It must be completed at a recognised clinic.
Ask the employer or visa authority which clinics are recognised, then obtain the medical report for the application.

## Visa fee

The published fees are 160 元/人 for a zero-entry visa, 206 元/人 for a one-entry visa for non-reciprocal countries, 313 元/人 for a two-entry visa for non-reciprocal countries, 413 元/人 for a half-year multiple-entry visa for non-reciprocal countries, and 619 元/人 for a one-year-or-longer multiple-entry visa for non-reciprocal countries.
Confirm which fee applies to the specific Z visa application before paying.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-27; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
