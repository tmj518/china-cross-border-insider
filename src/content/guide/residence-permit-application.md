This guide walks you through the residence permit application process after you arrive in China. It covers the documents, the medical check, and the submission steps. Follow each step in order to keep your stay lawful.

## Before You Start

Confirm your visa type and the purpose of your stay, as the residence permit category must match your visa. Gather your passport, a recent photo, and any documents your employer or school provides, such as an invitation letter or registration form.
Check whether your visa allows you to apply for a residence permit directly, or if you need to exit and re-enter with a different visa. Your sponsor can confirm this with the local exit-entry administration.

## Medical Examination

Most applicants must undergo a medical examination at a designated hospital or clinic. The exam includes a chest X-ray, blood tests, and a physical check. Bring your passport and two photos to the appointment.
The medical report is usually valid for a limited period, so schedule the exam close to your application date. Ask the clinic how long the report remains valid and plan accordingly.

## Prepare Your Application

Complete the residence permit application form, which you can get online or at the exit-entry administration office. Fill it out in black ink, and do not leave any fields blank.
Photocopy your passport's information page, visa page, and the page with your latest entry stamp. Also photocopy your accommodation registration form from the local police station, if you have one.

## Submit and Wait

Submit your application in person at the local exit-entry administration office. Bring all original documents and photocopies. You may need to pay a fee at the time of submission.
The processing time varies, but you will be given a receipt with a collection date. Do not travel outside the city or the country while your application is being processed, as this can delay or cancel it.

## Collect Your Permit

Return to the office on the collection date with your receipt and passport. Check the permit details immediately: your name, passport number, permit type, and validity dates.
If any detail is wrong, ask the officer to correct it before you leave. Once you have the permit, keep it with your passport and carry both when you travel within China.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-04; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
