If you stay somewhere other than a hotel, your accommodation must be registered with the local public security bureau. A hotel registers guests during check-in, but a private address does not create that registration automatically. The process below tells you what to do and what to keep for the residence-permit desk.

## Who Handles Registration

A foreigner staying somewhere other than a hotel is registered with the local public security bureau.
Hotels register their guests as part of check-in; a private address does not register the guest automatically.

## What the Registration Covers

The registration is tied to an address and a period.
If the address or period changes, the existing record should not be treated as covering the new stay.

## Where It Is Required

The registration is asked for at the residence-permit desk.
Keep the record you receive and take it with you to that appointment.

## Consequence of Not Registering

Failing to register is an offense punishable by a fine, and the fine is stated in the regulation.
A hotel registration cannot be used as a substitute for a private-address registration.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-27; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
