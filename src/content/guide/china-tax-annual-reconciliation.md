This guide covers the annual reconciliation filing for a tax year in China. It is separate from the amounts your employer withholds from salary each month. Use it to check whether a filing is required for your situation and to complete it within the official period.

## Understand the separate filing

Annual reconciliation is a filing made after the tax year ends. It is separate from the withholding your employer does each month, and it compares tax already withheld with the tax due on your income for that year.

## Check whether you must file

Whether a foreigner must file depends on income received, tax already withheld, and residence status during that tax year. Review these points against the tax authority's current reconciliation guidance before deciding that no filing is needed.

## Claim treaty relief in the return

Treaty relief is claimed in the reconciliation filing; it is not applied automatically. Complete the relevant claim parts and keep documents that show why you qualify.

## File during the authority window

The reconciliation window is set by the tax authority for a period each year and is not chosen by the taxpayer. Submit the filing during that window and keep the confirmation and result.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-27; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
