This guide walks you through the process of applying for a Chinese residence permit after you have arrived in China. It covers the documents, the application, and the collection. Follow the steps in order to avoid delays.

## Before You Apply

Ensure your passport is valid for the entire duration of your intended stay. Your employer or sponsor must provide a formal invitation letter and a copy of their business license.
You will need a recent passport-sized photo that meets Chinese specifications. Have your original passport, visa, and a completed application form ready.

## The Application Process

Submit your application in person at the local Exit and Entry Administration Bureau. You may need to book an appointment in advance.
The officer will review your documents and may ask for additional information. You will be given a receipt with a collection date.

## After Submission

Your application will be processed. You can track the status online using your receipt number.
When your permit is ready, return to the bureau with your receipt and passport to collect it. Check the details on the permit before leaving.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-09; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
