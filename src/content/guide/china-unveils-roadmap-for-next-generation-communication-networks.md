China has published a roadmap for next-generation communication networks. If you are relocating and your application touches telecom services, equipment or network access, the roadmap may change what you are asked to provide. This guide sets out the order of actions to take. It is not legal, tax or immigration advice.

## What the roadmap covers

The roadmap sets out planned direction for next-generation communication networks in China. It is a policy document, not a permit or a form you submit.
Because no figures from the roadmap could be verified against a source, this guide does not state dates, targets or thresholds. Treat any specific number you see elsewhere as unconfirmed until you check it at the source.
Your application may be affected only if it involves telecom services, network equipment, or access to communication infrastructure. If it does not, the roadmap is unlikely to change your procedure.

## Before you start

Confirm which authority handles your application. The roadmap does not replace the body that receives your paperwork.
Ask the receiving office, in writing, whether the roadmap changes the documents required for your application. Keep the reply.
Do not rely on this guide for any figure. Ask the office for the current requirements directly.

## After you submit

Keep a copy of everything you submit and note the date you submitted it.
If the office asks for additional documents, respond before any deadline they give you in writing.
If you are refused or asked to resubmit, ask for the reason in writing before you file again.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-27; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
