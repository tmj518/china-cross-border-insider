Your China residence permit number is a key identifier used in many official transactions. This guide shows you where to find it and how to use it correctly.

## What the Number Is

The residence permit number is printed on the physical card you received after your application was approved. It is not the same as your passport number or your visa number (if you previously held a visa).
The number is unique to your permit and is used by authorities to verify your legal stay in China.

## Where to Find It

Look at the front of your residence permit card. The number is typically printed in the top right corner, near your photo, or in the bottom section with other details.
If you have a newer card, the number may appear in a machine-readable zone at the bottom. Check both sides of the card if you cannot see it immediately.

## When You Need It

You will be asked for this number when registering for certain services, such as opening a bank account, signing a lease, or dealing with immigration matters.
Keep a photocopy or a digital scan of your permit card in a safe place, but do not share the number casually, as it is tied to your identity.
