China's second domestically built large cruise ship has started its first trial voyage. This guide explains what this means for you as a new resident and how to follow the news.

## What the Trial Voyage Means

The trial voyage is a testing phase before the ship enters regular service. It is not a public sailing, so you cannot book passage on it.
For relocation purposes, this signals the expansion of China's cruise industry, which may offer more travel options in the future.

## How to Stay Informed

Official announcements about the trial voyage and subsequent commercial sailings come from the cruise line and Chinese maritime authorities.
Check the cruise line's official website or social media channels for updates on when bookings will open.

## Planning Your Travel

If you plan to take a cruise after relocating, wait until the ship's commercial schedule is announced.
Consider other cruise ships already in service for your immediate travel needs.
