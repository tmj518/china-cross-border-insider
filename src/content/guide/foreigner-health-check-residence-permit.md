Before you can obtain or renew a residence permit in China, you must undergo a health check at a designated hospital. This guide walks you through the process, from scheduling to collecting your report, so you can avoid delays.

## Before You Go

Confirm which hospital is designated for foreigner health checks in your city. Your local exit-entry administration bureau or your employer can provide this information.
Prepare the required documents: your passport, a recent passport-sized photo, and the health check application form if provided by the authorities. Some hospitals may also ask for your residence permit application receipt.

## Scheduling the Appointment

Call the hospital or visit in person to book a time. Many hospitals have specific hours for foreigner health checks, often in the morning on weekdays.
Ask whether you need to fast before the blood test. If so, schedule for early morning and avoid eating or drinking anything except water for the required period before the appointment.

## The Examination Process

Arrive at the hospital with all your documents. You will likely be directed to a dedicated foreigner health check department.
The exam typically includes a physical examination, chest X-ray, blood test, and urine test. Follow the staff's instructions for each station.
The entire process usually takes a few hours, depending on the queue. Be prepared to wait between stations.

## Getting Your Results

After the exam, you will be told when to return to collect your health check report. This is usually within a few days, but the exact timing varies by hospital.
Bring your passport and any receipt given to you when you collect the report. Check that the report includes your name, photo, and a clear conclusion of 'fit' or 'qualified'.
Keep the original report safe; you will need it for your residence permit application. Make a copy for your records.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-29; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
