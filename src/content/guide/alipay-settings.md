When you arrive, Alipay is essential for paying for transport, food, and utilities. This guide walks you through the settings you need to adjust so the app works reliably with your foreign documents and local bank account. Complete these steps in order to avoid payment failures.

## Before You Start

You need a smartphone with the Alipay app installed and a local SIM card or Wi-Fi connection. Have your passport and your Chinese bank card ready, because you will enter both during setup.
If you already have an Alipay account from another country, you may need to switch the app region to China. Do this in the app's settings before proceeding.

## Verify Your Identity

Alipay requires identity verification to lift transaction limits and enable full features. Without it, you can only make small payments.
Use your passport for verification. The app will ask you to photograph the passport page and sometimes your face. Follow the on-screen prompts exactly.

## Link a Local Bank Card

A Chinese bank card is necessary for most transactions and for withdrawing cash. You can link a card from a major Chinese bank, but the card must be in your name.
Enter the card number, expiry date, and the phone number you registered with the bank. You will receive a text code to confirm.

## Set Payment Preferences

Choose which payment method is used first when you scan a merchant QR code. You can set the default to your bank card or to Alipay balance.
Enable or disable 'small-amount password-free payments' for quick transactions under a set limit. Decide based on your security comfort.

## Secure Your Account

Set a strong six-digit payment password and enable fingerprint or face unlock if your phone supports it. Also add a secondary verification method, like a second phone number or email.
Review your login devices and log out any you do not recognize. Check your transaction history regularly for unauthorized charges.
