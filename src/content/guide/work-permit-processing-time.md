Applying for a work permit in China involves several stages, each with its own processing time. This guide walks you through the procedure so you can plan your move without relying on fixed timelines.

## Before You Apply

Gather all required documents: your passport, a signed job offer, a medical examination report, and a copy of your employer's business license. Ensure your qualifications are authenticated by the Chinese embassy in your home country before you arrive.
Your employer must register with the local authorities and obtain a notification letter for you. This letter is essential for your visa application.

## Visa Application and Entry

Apply for a Z visa at the Chinese embassy or consulate in your home country. The processing time varies by consulate, so check their website for current procedures.
Once you receive the Z visa, enter China within its validity period. After entry, you must apply for the work permit and residence permit within the time allowed by your visa.

## Work Permit and Residence Permit

Your employer submits the work permit application to the local authorities. This step involves document review and can take several weeks.
After the work permit is approved, you must apply for a residence permit at the local public security bureau. This requires an in-person appointment and additional processing time.

## After Approval

Once you receive your residence permit, you can legally work and live in China. Keep your passport and permits safe, as you will need them for any future renewals.
Renewals must be initiated before the current permits expire. Start the renewal process well in advance to avoid gaps in your legal status.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-02; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
