This guide walks you through applying for a Home Return Permit while in Hong Kong. The process involves preparing documents, submitting an application, and collecting the permit. Follow the steps in order to avoid delays.

## Before You Apply

Check your eligibility: the permit is for Chinese citizens residing overseas who need to travel to mainland China.
Gather your original and photocopied identification documents, such as your passport and proof of Chinese nationality.
Prepare a recent passport-style photo that meets the specified size and background requirements.

## Submitting Your Application

Visit the designated China Travel Service (CTS) office in Hong Kong that handles Home Return Permit applications.
Complete the application form at the office or online beforehand, and bring the printed copy.
Submit your documents and photo at the counter, and pay the application fee at the cashier.

## After Submission

You will receive a receipt with a collection date. Keep this receipt safe.
The processing time varies; check the receipt for the expected collection date.
If additional documents are needed, the office will contact you. Respond promptly.

## Collecting Your Permit

Return to the same CTS office on the collection date with your receipt and original identification.
Verify the details on the permit before leaving the counter.
If you cannot collect in person, check if a proxy is allowed and what authorization is required.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-07; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
