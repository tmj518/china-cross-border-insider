This guide helps an employee organize a monthly China payroll review. It gives no rates or fixed sums because no verified figures were supplied. Follow the steps to identify the items that can change taxable pay and later filings.

## Progressive Tax

The tax is progressive, so the rate on the last unit of income is not the rate on the whole salary. Applying that final rate to total pay would therefore give the wrong result.

## Residence Status

Residence status decides whether worldwide income is in scope, and that status turns on days in China. Establish your status before deciding which income must be included.

## Salary and Reimbursements

Allowances that are paid as reimbursements are treated differently from the same money paid as salary. Keep reimbursement claims and supporting documents separate from payroll records.

## Withholding and Reconciliation

The employer withholds monthly; the annual reconciliation is a separate filing. Monthly withholding may not be the final position, because the reconciliation can produce an additional payment or a refund.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-27; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
