If you are relocating to China and have heard about the APEC Business Travel Card, treat it as a document to verify, not a shortcut to assume. This procedure tells you what to check and in what order. It is not legal, tax or immigration advice, and no figure here has been verified against a source.

## What this card is and is not

The APEC Business Travel Card is issued through your home economy, not through China. It is meant for short business visits, not for living or working in China.
It does not replace a work permit, a residence permit or a visa for employment. If your move involves a job, a business you run locally or a long stay, the card is not the document that authorises it.
Because the fact sheet for this topic is empty, this guide gives no processing times, no fees and no validity periods. Get those from the issuing authority in your own economy.

## Check eligibility in your home economy

Not every economy takes part, and rules differ between those that do. Start with the government office in your own economy that handles APEC cards.
Ask whether you qualify as a business traveller and what evidence they require. Do not rely on what a colleague or a relocation agent tells you.
If your economy does not issue the card, stop here and plan your China entry through the normal visa route.

## Separate the card from your China stay

The card covers short business entry. Your actual relocation needs a visa or permit that matches what you will do in China.
Decide which of those you need before you apply for anything, because the card does not change the requirements for working or residing.
Keep the two processes separate in your planning and in your paperwork.

## Confirm details with official sources only

Confirm current rules with the issuing authority in your economy and with the Chinese embassy or consulate that covers your place of residence.
Ask each office the same questions and compare the answers. Where they differ, follow the stricter one.
Rules change. Check again close to your travel date rather than relying on what you read earlier.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-17; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
