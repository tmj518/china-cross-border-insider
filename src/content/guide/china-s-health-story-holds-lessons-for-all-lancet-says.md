The Lancet commentary on China's health story is being read by people deciding whether to move. This guide gives you a procedure for using that commentary without treating it as a promise about your own health cover or costs. It is not legal, tax or immigration advice.

## What the commentary is and is not

The Lancet piece is a commentary on China's health story. It is not a schedule of benefits, a price list or a rulebook for foreigners.
Read it as background on how the system is described, not as a statement of what you will receive. Nothing in this guide is verified against a source, so treat every claim in the commentary as something to check locally.

## Before you move

Collect the documents your employer or school says you need for health cover. Ask which provider handles your cover and what it includes.
Write down the questions the commentary raises for you, and take them to the provider rather than answering them yourself.

## After you arrive

Register with the provider named in your paperwork. Keep the receipt and any card you are given.
Find the nearest clinic that accepts your cover and confirm it in person before you need it.

## When something goes wrong

Contact your provider first, before you commit to treatment, and ask what is covered.
Keep every receipt and record. If a claim is refused, ask the provider to put the reason in writing.
