China's third APEC hosting comes with talk of opening-up's next frontier, but the fact sheet for this topic is empty. No figure here can be checked against a source, so this guide gives you the order of actions only. Treat every number you see elsewhere as unconfirmed until you verify it yourself.

## What the announcement does and does not change

The APEC secretariat chief's remarks point to opening-up as the next frontier. That is a signal about direction, not a published rule you can act on today.
Nothing in the supplied material gives a date, a fee, a quota or a processing time. Do not plan your move around a number you have not seen on an official page.

## Where to check before you commit money

Use the official immigration and foreign-affairs channels for your city and for the national level. Read the current notice, not a reposted summary.
If a page shows a figure, note where it came from and when it was posted. If you cannot find the source, do not rely on it.

## How to sequence your move

Start with the permit or visa category that matches your purpose, then work backward to the documents it requires. Do not book travel or sign a lease until the category is confirmed in writing.
Keep copies of every submission and every receipt. When a rule changes, the copy is what lets you show what applied to you.

## What to do when a figure is missing

Ask the issuing office directly, in writing, and keep the reply. A written answer from the office that handles your case is worth more than any guide.
If the answer is still unclear, delay the spending step rather than guess. A wrong guess costs you the fee and the time.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-14; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
