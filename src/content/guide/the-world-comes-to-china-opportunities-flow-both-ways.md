This guide covers the procedure for planning a move to China when the official figures for your route are not yet in hand. It does not price anything, because no figure in this topic could be verified against a source. Treat every step as a way to find the real number from the authority that issues it, not as a number itself.

## What this procedure covers

The world comes to China and opportunities flow both ways, but the paperwork runs in one direction at a time: you apply, you wait, you comply. This procedure lists the actions in the order you perform them, from choosing a route to keeping your status valid after arrival.
No figure in this topic could be verified against a source, so this guide contains no amounts, no counts and no deadlines. Where a step would normally carry a number, the step tells you which office or document holds that number and tells you to read it there.

## Why there are no figures here

Fees, processing times and validity periods change and differ by city, by category and by nationality. A number copied from an old guide or a forum post can cost you money or time, so this guide does not repeat any.
Your only reliable figure is the one printed on the official notice, the application form or the receipt you are given. Ask for it in writing and keep it.

## How to use the steps

Work through the steps in order. Do not pay anyone or book travel until the step that confirms your category is complete.
If an office gives you a figure that differs from what you were told earlier, treat the written figure from the issuing office as the one that applies and ask them to confirm it in writing.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-10; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
