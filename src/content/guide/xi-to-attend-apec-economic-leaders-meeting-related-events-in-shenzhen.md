President Xi will attend APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting events in Shenzhen. If you are relocating to or living in Shenzhen, this affects your schedule and mobility. Here is what to do to stay informed and prepared.

## Understand the Event Scope

The APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting brings high-level officials and security to Shenzhen. Expect road closures, venue restrictions, and increased police presence near conference sites.
Your daily commute may be rerouted, and some areas may be temporarily off-limits. This is temporary, but planning ahead reduces stress.

## Monitor Official Announcements

Local authorities will publish notices about road closures, public transport changes, and security checks. Follow the Shenzhen government's official social media accounts and local news.
Also check your employer's communication channels; many companies adjust work hours or remote work during major events.

## Prepare Your Documents

Carry your passport or Chinese ID at all times. Security checks may occur at random in the city center.
If you are a foreign resident, ensure your visa or residence permit is valid. Renewal offices may have limited hours during the event.

## Adjust Your Travel Plans

Avoid non-essential travel to the conference venue area. Use public transport instead of driving, as parking and roads near the venue will be restricted.
If you must travel, leave earlier than usual. Allow extra time for security screening at airports, train stations, and metro stops.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-11-14; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
