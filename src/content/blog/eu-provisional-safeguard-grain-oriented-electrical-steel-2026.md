> **Safeguard measure** — a trade defence instrument the EU applies when increased imports of a product cause or threaten serious injury to EU producers. Unlike anti-dumping duties, which target specific countries and unfair pricing, safeguard measures apply to imports from all origins and respond to import surges regardless of whether the exporting country acted unfairly.
