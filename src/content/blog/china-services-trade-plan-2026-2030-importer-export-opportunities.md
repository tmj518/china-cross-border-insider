## What happened

The Ministry of Commerce unveiled China's services trade development plan for 2026-2030, as reported by Xinhua on 27 November 2026. The plan sets the direction for services trade over the five-year period. The same day, China Daily reported on China's push to increase services exports.

The figures behind the plan show the current state of services trade. In the first three quarters of 2026, China's imports and exports of services reached 5,768.77 billion yuan (about 859.78 billion U.S. dollars), up 8.5 percent year on year, according to the Ministry of Commerce via Xinhua. China Daily reported the same period's total at 5.77 trillion yuan ($866 billion), also up 8.5 percent.

The deficit in services trade came in at 1,225.97 billion yuan in the first three quarters of 2026, according to the Ministry of Commerce via Xinhua. China's services imports reached more than 620 billion U.S. dollars in 2025, according to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange via Xinhua.

Services exports rose 14.8 percent to 2.27 trillion yuan in the first three quarters of 2026, with knowledge-intensive services accounting for 53.7 percent of the total, according to the Ministry of Commerce via China Daily. Offshore R&D services rose 8.3 percent to 86.56 billion yuan in the same period.

> **Services trade** — the import and export of services rather than goods. This includes transport, travel, financial services, telecommunications, computer and information services, intellectual property charges, and research and development services. China's services trade plan for 2026-2030 sets targets for growth and market opening in these sectors.
