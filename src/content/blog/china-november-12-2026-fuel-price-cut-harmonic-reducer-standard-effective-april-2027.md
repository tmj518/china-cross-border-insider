## Definition

China's National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) cut domestic gasoline and diesel standard-product prices by 645 yuan and 625 yuan per tonne respectively, effective from 24:00 on November 12, and the revised national harmonic drive reducer standards GB/T 12601-2026 and GB/T 14118-2026 take effect on April 1, 2027.

> "From 24:00 on November 12, domestic gasoline and diesel (standard product) prices are reduced by 645 yuan and 625 yuan per tonne respectively."
> — National Development and Reform Commission, November 12, 2026

## What happened

The NDRC announced that from 24:00 on November 12, domestic gasoline and diesel (standard product) prices fall by 645 yuan and 625 yuan per tonne respectively. This is a direct reduction in the fuel component of any shipment staged inside China before export. The People's Bank of China now conducts daily overnight reverse repurchase operations at an annual interest rate of 1.25 percent, starting November 2, 2026, with institutions bidding for the amounts they require each working day.

Separately, the revised national harmonic drive reducer standards GB/T 12601-2026 and GB/T 14118-2026 take effect on April 1, 2027, according to China's National Standard Information Public Service Platform. China's harmonic reducer market was valued at approximately 3.8 billion yuan in 2025 and is expected to reach 4.8 billion yuan in 2026, according to estimates by industry research firm Zhiyan Consulting.

## Why now

The fuel price adjustment and the standard revision arrive together on November 12, 2026. The NDRC sets fuel prices on a schedule tied to international crude movements, and this cut reflects the current pricing cycle. The harmonic reducer standard revision was published ahead of its April 1, 2027 effective date, giving importers a defined window to verify that incoming shipments conform to the revised requirements. The PBOC's shift to daily overnight reverse repos at 1.25 percent signals a deliberate easing of short-term yuan funding conditions.

## So what

For an importer, the fuel price cut lowers the per-kilometre cost of moving goods through China by road or rail. A 645 yuan per tonne cut on gasoline and a 625 yuan per tonne cut on diesel translate directly into lower logistics budgets for any shipment staged inside China. The harmonic reducer standard revision means importers who source these precision motion-control components from Chinese suppliers must verify that incoming shipments conform to GB/T 12601-2026 and GB/T 14118-2026 before April 1, 2027, because non-compliant units arriving after that date face rejection at customs or rework at the supplier's cost. The PBOC's 1.25 percent overnight reverse repo rate anchors short-term yuan borrowing costs, which affects the cost of financing inventory held in yuan.

## FAQ

**What does the fuel price cut mean for my logistics costs?**

The NDRC cut gasoline by 645 yuan per tonne and diesel by 625 yuan per tonne effective from 24:00 on November 12. For an importer moving goods through China, this lowers the fuel component of domestic trucking and rail freight. On a 100-tonne shipment, the gasoline cut saves 64,500 yuan and the diesel cut saves 62,500 yuan. On a 500-tonne shipment, the savings reach 322,500 yuan for gasoline and 312,500 yuan for diesel. These savings apply to the fuel consumed in moving goods inside China before export, so importers should reprice their logistics budgets to reflect the lower fuel cost. The exact saving depends on the fuel mix of the carrier and the distance travelled, but the per-tonne cuts are the anchor for any recalculation.

**When do the new harmonic reducer standards take effect?**

The revised national harmonic drive reducer standards GB/T 12601-2026 and GB/T 14118-2026 take effect on April 1, 2027, according to China's National Standard Information Public Service Platform. Importers who source harmonic drive reducers from Chinese suppliers have until that date to verify that incoming shipments conform to the revised standards. The market affected is substantial: China's harmonic reducer market was valued at approximately 3.8 billion yuan in 2025 and is expected to reach 4.8 billion yuan in 2026, according to Zhiyan Consulting. Importers should request certificates of conformity from suppliers now and schedule any necessary re-testing before the effective date.

**What happens if my harmonic reducer shipment does not meet the new standards?**

Shipments of harmonic drive reducers that do not conform to GB/T 12601-2026 and GB/T 14118-2026 after April 1, 2027 face rejection at customs or rework at the supplier's cost. The compliance deadline is firm, and the standard revision affects a market valued at 3.8 billion yuan in 2025. Importers should audit their current supplier base against the revised standards, request updated test reports, and confirm that any units already in transit or in inventory will meet the new requirements by the effective date. The cost of non-compliance includes customs delays, storage charges, and the expense of returning or reworking non-conforming units, all of which exceed the cost of verifying compliance in advance.
