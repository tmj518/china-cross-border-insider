feat: a CSP self-check, so a policy that blocks the site's own scripts cannot ship

The previous commit fixed the policy; this makes the failure impossible to ship again.
The check is one line of reasoning: for every built page, hash each inline script and
confirm script-src carries it.

It lives in preflight, after the build, because dist/ is what it inspects.

Getting the check itself right took three attempts, all on the same mistake of being
clever about quotes. The policy value is wrapped in double quotes and is itself full of
single quotes ('self', 'unsafe-inline'), so a [^"']* character class stops at the
opening quote of 'self' and captures twelve characters. Two versions reported all 36
pages as broken while the browser was happily running the menu. The working version
matches the attribute's own quote character and lets the content run to the matching
one.

Also moves frame-ancestors out of the meta policy and into _headers, where browsers
actually honour it; delivered through a meta element it is ignored and warns on every
page load.

Preflight now reports: csp PASS, all 32 scripts parse, 22/22 articles built.
