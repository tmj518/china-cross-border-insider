feat: a reviewer agent to stand in for the human read the spec rules out

初心与目标.md says 不人工审稿 and that the two gates decide instead. The gates
cannot decide everything a reader decides, and the gap was measured rather than
assumed: three defects reached the built site that neither gate caught. A subtitle
at 167 characters that failed the whole build. An article with no outbound link.
And the de minimis piece covering 19 CFR 145.12(a)(2)(vi) while missing (v), and
treating the bond as the main new requirement when the rule's harder constraint is
that only a licensed customs broker can file.

The third is the one that matters, because it is not a formatting error or a missing
field. It is a check that an article's claims match its own sources, which is what a
human reviewer does and what no tool here did.

So tools/reviewer-check.mjs checks five classes: a number in the body not backed by
any figure or source, an AI summary that has drifted from the body, a data point
stating a figure the article never establishes, date consistency between reviewBy
and the dates the piece is about, and claims about this site's own process that the
frontmatter does not support. Grades are advisory except must-fix, which sets the
exit code. It is wired into preflight, so it runs on every unattended pass.

It earned its place on the first run. It found that the AI summary of the CBAM
cost article stated EUR 540 for grain-oriented electrical steel while the body and
the figure list contained no such figure — the exact class it exists for. The model
prices that line at EUR 539.91, so the claim was right and unsupported, which is the
worst combination: a reader could not have checked it. Fixed by adding the figure
with its five-part record and by making the summary state the precise value.

Four of my own bugs on the way to a clean run, each found by testing the check
rather than trusting it. The AI-summary comparison searched the body including the
summary, which made it vacuous. A length threshold raised to five digits to exclude
a bare year also excluded every three-digit figure, so an injected EUR 999 passed
unseen. A substring test compared "18000" against a body that writes "18,000". And
the callout check did not allow a stated annual figure to be divided into a
quarterly one.

Verified by injecting the defect each rule is for and confirming it fires with the
right severity and exit code, with every mutation checked on disk before the tool
runs and the restore verified by re-running rather than assumed: four of four
caught, and the library clean again afterwards. 20 articles, 0 must-fix, 0
should-fix.
