LANDED MATH — FACT DOSSIER
Research date: 2026-10-01 (Asia/Shanghai)
Topic: 2026 development changing cost/lead time of moving goods out of China (sea/air)

====================================================================
1. HEADLINE
====================================================================
On 18 June 2026 the "Shanghai Shipping Index" container benchmarks changed
compiling/publishing body: the China Containerized Freight Index (CCFI) and
Shanghai Containerized Freight Index (SCFI) — the two indices used as the
reference baseline in China ocean-freight contracts and surcharge formulas —
moved from Shanghai Shipping Exchange to the newly established Shanghai
Shipping Research Institute (in Preparation), with the newly published
2026-09-30 readings showing CCFI composite at 1923.93 (+0.3% w/w) against
SCFI composite at 3662.30 (-24.32 points w/w — i.e. index levels moving in
opposite directions on the same day).

====================================================================
2. WHY IT MATTERS TO AN IMPORTER
====================================================================
CCFI/SCFI are the reference indices written into carrier service contracts,
BAF/LSS (bunker) adjustment formulas and rate-review clauses; when the
publishing entity, its copyright holder and its website all change mid-year,
a buyer auditing a surcharge pass-through has to re-point every reference to
the new publisher, or lose the ability to verify what the agreed baseline was.
The same-day divergence between the two benchmarks makes that traceability
problem concrete: on 2026-09-30 the CCFI composite rose 0.3% while the SCFI
composite fell 24.32 points, and the CCFI lane detail shows a 9.1% jump in the
Southeast Asia lane to 1322.15 against a 2.9% fall in the Europe lane to
1970.38 — so importers on different trades saw opposite cost directions in the
same publication week, and lane-level figures are proving hard to verify
externally (see NOT FOUND).
