research: two studies, their verification, and a consolidated action list

Three research deliverables, all requested by the operator, plus the verification that
makes them usable rather than merely read.

对标分析_两家最强同行.md — the two best-executed sites in adjacent territory, chosen
with reasons and with the nine rejected candidates explained. Our World in Data for
verifiability, Trivium China for the business model, because this site has exactly two
vital signs and one of them is which of those two it resembles. The single
highest-value finding: OWID gives every figure its own URL - 11 grapher links and 9
distinct chart URLs in one article - while our 195 figure records have none. Our
advantage is that each of ours already carries a five-part record including a retrieval
date, which OWID has no field for.

谷歌官方SEO学习总结.md — Google's own documentation only, 43 pages whose full text is
kept under the ignored _research/gdocs for re-checking every quote. The substantive
surprises: the sitelinks search box was removed in November 2024, so adding SearchAction
today accomplishes nothing; priority and changefreq in a sitemap are ignored outright;
canonical is a hint rather than a rule; and Article and Organization structured data have
literally no required properties, which means our missing image and logo are lost
opportunities rather than violations. Only BreadcrumbList has hard requirements. The
study also declines to invent Core Web Vitals numbers when the API rate-limited it, and
says so.

行动清单_两份研究.md — the cross-cutting list, ordered by return rather than by the
documents' own structure, split into configuration-level work under two days,
structural work over one to two weeks, and monetisation. It names what not to copy and
why, including the finding that neither competitor uses HTML tables at all, so our
per-unit tables are an original form rather than an imitation and should not be removed
in the name of following best practice.

Two verification documents, docs/SEO缺陷独立核实.md and docs/对标研究核实.md, because a
subagent's findings are claims and every one of them about this site is checkable. All
confirmed: www serves 200 without redirecting, no HSTS, no lastmod anywhere in the
sitemap, no content images at all, no max-image-preview, og-default.png is 1200x630
rather than 16:9, HTML is not edge-cached while the CSS is, the subscription form is a
mailto with the operator's own note that one click is not wired up, 195 figures with no
URLs, no citation metadata, no licence, and five page types with no structured data.

The verification also found something neither study did: the Article JSON-LD emits no
dateModified at all, even though the rule for it was specified and implemented elsewhere
in the site. That is a one-line gap rather than a design question.

The competitor study's claim of a 22 KB page turned out to be 26.9 KB of HTML plus
17.6 KB of CSS measured here - still far below OWID's 155 to 389 KB, with zero external
scripts.

Removed tools/gdoc_fetch.ps1, a throwaway fetch helper the study wrote.
