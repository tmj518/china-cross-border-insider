## So what

The cost of a container does not move because a diplomatic article was published. It moves when a customs schedule, a ministry notice or a chamber circular changes a rate. None of those changed here.

The assumption is expensive in both directions. An importer who raises a landed-cost estimate on the strength of a diplomatic article overprices a shipment and loses the order. An importer who lowers one on the same strength underprices it and absorbs the difference.

The discipline is to separate the class of document from the class of number. An authored article is not one of those channels.
