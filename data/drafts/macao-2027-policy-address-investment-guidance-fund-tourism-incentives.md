## Definition

## What happened

The address was delivered on Nov. 16, 2026 and targets the 2027 policy year. The macro backdrop it cites is real GDP growth of 2.9 percent year-on-year across the first three quarters.

Three instruments are named:

- **An investment guidance fund.** The fund is aimed at attracting projects to the Guangdong-Macao In-Depth Cooperation Zone in Hengqin. For a supplier, Hengqin is the address that matters: a project located in the zone is the thing the fund is built to pull in.
- **Inbound tourism incentives.** The target source markets named are Europe, India and the Middle East. Incentives aimed at those markets change the cost of reaching a Macao buyer from those places, not from elsewhere.
- **A payment-rail link.** The government will develop a link between the digital pataca and the digital yuan. Settlement rails are a cost line for anyone invoicing across the mainland-Macao border.

## Why now

The address is the annual statement of intent for the year ahead, and it lands while Macao's economy is growing: 2.9 percent year-on-year real growth across the first three quarters is the figure the address itself uses to frame the 2027 plan. A government writing a growth plan is a government with room to spend on attraction, and the three instruments named are where it says that spending goes.

## So what

The address does not publish a rate, a subsidy percentage or a fund size. What it publishes is a direction, and a direction is enough to change where a supplier puts a proposal.

If you are quoting into Macao, the practical question the address raises is whether your buyer or your project sits inside Hengqin. The fund is described as targeting that zone for project attraction, so a proposal framed around a Hengqin location is framed around the instrument the government says it will use.

If you are selling into Macao's visitor economy, the named source markets are Europe, India and the Middle East. A supplier already holding distribution in those markets has a reason to put a Macao proposal in front of a partner there.

If you invoice across the mainland-Macao boundary, the digital pataca and digital yuan link is the rail to watch. It is described as something the government will develop, not something already operating.
