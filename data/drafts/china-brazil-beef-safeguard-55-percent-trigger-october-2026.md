## So what

The cost consequence is a rate stack, not a single duty. The 55% is added on top of the applicable MFN rate, so the effective rate on Brazilian beef is the MFN rate plus 55 percentage points.

```
effective rate = MFN rate + 55%
```

The record does not give the MFN rate for the beef lines in question, so the effective rate cannot be stated as a single number here. What the record does establish is the structure: whatever the MFN rate is, add 55 percentage points to it once the add-on is live.

For an importer, the exposure to plan for is the difference between the pre-trigger rate and the post-trigger rate on the same shipment. That difference is 55 percentage points of the customs value, applied on top of the MFN duty already owed.
