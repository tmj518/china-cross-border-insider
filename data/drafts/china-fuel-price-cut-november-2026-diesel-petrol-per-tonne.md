## AI Summary

China's National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) cut domestic petrol prices by 645 yuan per tonne and diesel prices by 625 yuan per tonne, effective from 24:00 on 12 November 2026. The adjustment lowers the wholesale ceiling prices that fuel distributors and logistics operators pay. For importers moving goods within China, this reduces the diesel component of trucking and rail freight costs. The exact saving per shipment depends on fuel consumption, distance, and vehicle type.

## What happened

The National Development and Reform Commission issued a price adjustment notice on 12 November 2026, reducing the domestic prices of petrol and diesel (standard products) by 645 yuan per tonne and 625 yuan per tonne respectively. The new prices take effect from 24:00 on 12 November 2026.

The notice states: "自11月12日24时起，国内汽、柴油（标准品）价格每吨分别下调645元、625元" — from 24:00 on 12 November, domestic petrol and diesel (standard products) prices are reduced by 645 yuan and 625 yuan per tonne respectively.

This is a ceiling price adjustment. The NDRC sets the maximum wholesale and retail prices for refined fuel products in China. The 645 yuan and 625 yuan figures represent the reduction in the ceiling price per tonne, not the final pump price. Provincial-level pricing bureaus then adjust local retail prices within the national ceiling framework.

The adjustment applies to standard products. Non-standard grades of petrol and diesel are priced by provincial authorities in relation to the standard product price, following the quality-based pricing rules that the NDRC has established. The relationship between standard and non-standard grades is set out in the same notice framework.

> **Standard product** — in China's fuel pricing system, the reference grade of petrol or diesel against which the NDRC sets its national ceiling price. Other grades are priced at a differential to the standard product, set by provincial pricing authorities within the national framework. The 645 yuan and 625 yuan per tonne reductions apply to the standard products; other grades move by the corresponding differential.

## Why now

The NDRC adjusts domestic fuel prices in response to changes in international crude oil prices, following the pricing mechanism that links domestic refined product prices to a basket of international crude benchmarks. The adjustment on 12 November 2026 reflects the movement in international crude prices over the preceding ten working days, the window the pricing mechanism uses.

The notice does not state the international price level that triggered the adjustment. The NDRC's pricing mechanism operates on a formula that averages international crude prices over a ten-working-day period and adjusts domestic prices when the change exceeds a threshold. The 645 yuan and 625 yuan per tonne reductions indicate a downward movement in the international benchmark basket over the relevant window.

## So what

For an importer, the fuel price cut reduces the cost of moving goods inside China. Diesel is the dominant fuel for trucking, and the 625 yuan per tonne reduction in the diesel ceiling price flows through to transport operators' fuel bills. The size of the saving depends on how much diesel a given shipment consumes.

A full truckload (FTL) moving goods from a factory in Guangdong to a port in Shenzhen covers roughly 150 kilometres. A heavy truck consumes approximately 30 litres of diesel per 100 kilometres on a loaded run, so the trip uses about 45 litres. Diesel weighs approximately 0.84 kilograms per litre, so 45 litres is about 37.8 kilograms, or 0.0378 tonnes. At the 625 yuan per tonne reduction, the saving on that single leg is about 23.6 yuan.

The same calculation scales with distance. A shipment from a factory in the Yangtze River Delta to Shanghai port, roughly 300 kilometres, uses about 90 litres of diesel, or 0.0756 tonnes. The saving is about 47.3 yuan. A longer domestic leg — say from Chengdu to Shanghai, roughly 1,900 kilometres — uses about 570 litres, or 0.479 tonnes, saving about 299 yuan.

These are small numbers per shipment. The fuel price cut matters more for importers who run their own fleets or who negotiate freight contracts with a fuel surcharge clause. A fleet of 50 trucks each running 2,000 kilometres per week consumes about 25,200 litres of diesel per week, or 21.2 tonnes. At 625 yuan per tonne, the weekly saving is about 13,250 yuan, or about 689,000 yuan per year.

For importers who use third-party carriers, the saving is indirect. Trucking companies absorb the fuel cost reduction into their operating costs. Whether the saving reaches the importer depends on the freight contract. Contracts with a fuel adjustment clause will pass the reduction through; fixed-rate contracts will not, at least until renegotiation.

The reduction also affects rail freight. China's rail network uses diesel for non-electrified lines, though the majority of freight is on electrified lines. The diesel price cut has a smaller effect on rail than on road transport.

Importers should also consider the timing. The adjustment takes effect at 24:00 on 12 November 2026. Fuel purchased before that time is at the old price; fuel purchased after is at the new price. Transport operators who fill tanks before the deadline gain nothing, because the price cut is a ceiling reduction, not a one-off rebate.

## FAQ

**What does the NDRC fuel price adjustment mean for my freight costs?**

The NDRC sets ceiling prices for refined fuel products in China. The 625 yuan per tonne diesel reduction lowers the maximum price that fuel retailers and wholesalers can charge. For a trucking company, this reduces the cost of diesel, which is the main fuel for road freight. Whether the saving reaches you depends on your freight contract. If the contract has a fuel adjustment clause, the reduction passes through. If the contract is fixed-rate, the carrier keeps the saving until the contract is renegotiated. The per-tonne figure is the reference point for calculating the size of the saving for any given fuel consumption.

**How do I calculate the saving for a specific shipment?**

The saving depends on the diesel consumption of the vehicle and the distance travelled. A heavy truck consumes roughly 30 litres per 100 kilometres on a loaded run. Diesel weighs about 0.84 kilograms per litre. For a 300-kilometre leg, the truck uses about 90 litres, or 0.0756 tonnes. At 625 yuan per tonne, the saving is about 47.3 yuan. The NDRC notice provides only the per-tonne price reduction.

**When does the new price take effect?**

The adjustment takes effect at 24:00 on 12 November 2026. Fuel purchased before that time is at the old ceiling price; fuel purchased after is at the new, lower ceiling price. The NDRC notice sets the effective time explicitly. There is no grace period and no retroactive application. Transport operators who fill tanks before the deadline do not gain anything, because the reduction is a ceiling price change, not a rebate on fuel already purchased.
